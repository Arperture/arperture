Honest answers

Questions we'd ask too.

What's the catch with the free website?

There isn't one — but there is a business model, and we're happy to say it out loud. Building your site free is our marketing budget: instead of spending it on ads, we spend it proving we're good. We make our money on the monthly Care Plan, and we only keep that if the results keep earning it. That's why there's no contract.

What exactly do I own?

Your domain, your content, and your website — full stop. The domain is registered in your name from day one. If you ever cancel, we hand over the complete site files within 7 days and you can host them anywhere.

How big a site is included?

The free build covers up to 5 pages on the Arperture design system — which fits the vast majority of local businesses. Need more (online ordering, booking systems, large catalogs)? We'll quote that part separately and transparently.

How fast will I rank #1 on Google?

Nobody honest can promise you that — and you should be wary of anyone who does. SEO is a compounding investment: meaningful movement typically shows in 3–6 months, and your monthly report tracks progress from day one. Our satisfaction guarantee applies to the website itself, which we control completely.

What's "AI Visibility" and why should I care?

More and more of your customers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI for recommendations instead of scrolling search results. If AI doesn't know your business exists, you're invisible to those customers. Our flagship plan tracks whether AI engines mention you and engineers your site so they do.
