## Niche
AI for small business — practical AI tools, automations, and workflows that help
small business owners and solopreneurs save time, cut costs, and make more money.
Not theory, not hype — show the thing that actually works.

## ICP — who you're talking to
- Small business owners, solopreneurs, and operators (agencies, local services,
  e-commerce, creators-as-businesses) who know AI matters but feel behind.
- They want: more output without more headcount, automations that run while they
  sleep, an edge over bigger competitors.
- They fear: wasting money on the wrong tool, looking like AI slop, falling
  behind, and that "AI is too technical for me."
- The pain you solve: turning the overwhelming AI landscape into a few concrete,
  do-this-today moves.

- Tone: direct, practical, energetic, no-fluff. Friendly expert who's actually
  built the thing, not a hype account.

  "Stop doing X, do Y instead."
- Avoid: buzzword soup, vague futurism, fear-mongering, "game-changer/synergy"
