# What makes a viral "yap"-style short — research notes

Distilled from research on high-performing short-form talking-head ("yap")
content on Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. This is the knowledge
base the skill draws on; update it as you learn what works for *your* audience.

## The format in one line
A single person talking directly to camera, fast and dense, delivering one
sharp idea with a strong hook and a memorable closing line. No b-roll required;
the words and the face do the work.

## Hooks (the first 1–3 seconds decide everything)
Proven hook patterns:
- **Contrarian / pattern-interrupt:** "Everyone's doing X. That's exactly why
  they're invisible." Take the popular advice and flip it.
- **Curiosity gap:** "Here's what nobody's telling you about X."
- **Callout to the ICP:** "If you're a [audience] still doing X, stop."
- **Stakes/loss:** "Your 2026 [thing] strategy won't work, and here's why."
- **Number/specific promise:** "3 things I'd never do if I were starting over."

Rules: lead with the hook (no intro, no "hey guys"), make a claim, create an
open loop the rest of the video closes.

## Topic selection — engineer for the ACTION you want
Don't optimize for likes. Optimize for the behavior:
- **Saves:** dense, reference-worthy, "screenshot this."
- **Shares / DMs:** "send this to someone who…", relatable callouts, rants.
- **Comments:** mild controversy, a question, a take people want to argue with.
Pick topics that score high on: **DM-shareability × contrarian angle × ICP pain.**

## Virality mechanics
- **Density over duration.** Cut every word that isn't load-bearing.
- **One idea per video.** Don't teach three things; nail one.
- **Authenticity is the moat.** In a feed flooded with AI slop, a real opinion,
  a lived story, and a specific point of view are the things AI content can't
  fake. Train the avatar on *your* voice, beliefs, and experience so it doesn't
  sound like everyone else.
- **Button line.** End on a quotable one-liner that recontextualizes the hook.
- **Pace/energy.** Slightly faster delivery (≈1.1×) reads as higher energy.

## Anti-patterns
- Slow intros, throat-clearing, "in this video I'm going to…"
- Hedging and corporate filler.
- Trying to cover too much.
- Generic takes with no personal angle (sounds like AI; gets ignored).

## Worked example (from the reference video)
> Everyone's using AI to pump out more content. That's exactly why they're
> invisible. Here's what nobody's saying. The feed is drowning in AI spam, so
> the algorithm stopped rewarding volume and started rewarding the one thing AI
> can't fake — you. Your real opinion. The story only you have lived. When you
> use AI to post more, you blend in. When you use it to say something true
> faster, you stand out. AI isn't your content. It's your leverage. Use it to
> amplify a real voice, not replace one.

Why it works: contrarian hook → names the mechanism → reframe → button line
that flips the premise. One idea, ~110 words, every line load-bearing.
