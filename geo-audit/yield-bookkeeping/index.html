② Findings by Severity

Critical & High

  • Critical AI crawlers are blocked at the door

    The homepage returns HTTP 403 Forbidden to any non-browser user-agent (re-confirmed 20 Jun 2026 after the WordPress security plugins were disabled — the block persists). The filtering is therefore upstream of WordPress: a CDN/host firewall, a "block AI bots / bad bot" toggle, or server-level ModSecurity rules are rejecting GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and CCBot.

    Impact: AI engines can't read your pages, so they cite directories about you instead of you — and never quote your services, pricing, or differentiators directly.

  • Critical Zero share-of-voice on non-branded commercial queries

    For the highest-value prompts ("best remote bookkeeping for small business"), Yield does not appear at all. These are the prompts that win new, non-local clients — exactly the nationwide audience Yield markets to.

    Impact: competitors with crawlable, content-rich location/topic pages capture the AI recommendation by default.

  • High No detectable answer-ready / structured content

    No FAQ schema, comparison pages, or quotable statistics could be detected (and can't be verified while the site blocks crawlers). Generative engines preferentially quote concise, structured, fact-dense passages.

    Impact: even once crawlable, thin/un-structured pages are less likely to be extracted into answers.

  • High No llms.txt / unverifiable sitemap

    An llms.txt guide for AI agents is absent, and sitemap.xml / robots.txt could not be confirmed (403). Engines lack a clean map of what to read.
