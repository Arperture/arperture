## What this audit measures

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of getting a business
**named, cited, and recommended inside AI-generated answers** — ChatGPT,
Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, and Copilot. Unlike classic SEO
(ranking a blue link), GEO is about whether the AI *mentions you* when a
customer asks it a question, and whether it frames you favorably.

For local home services, the buyer journey increasingly starts with a prompt
like *"who's the best plumber near me for a water heater leak?"* — and the
answer the AI gives is the shortlist. If you're not in it, you don't get the call.

**Methodology.** We simulated the real questions Loudoun-area homeowners type
into answer engines, across four intent categories (branded, "best plumber"
discovery, service-specific, and emergency), and recorded whether Artisan
surfaced, how it was framed, and which competitors won. We cross-referenced the
off-site citation and review footprint that AI engines draw from.

## Executive summary

Artisan Plumbing has a **strong brand and a genuinely good reputation** — and
it shows up clearly when someone already knows your name or asks the narrow
question *"best plumber in Loudoun County."* You own that lane.

The problem is everything one step earlier in the funnel. On the
**service-intent and emergency queries that actually generate calls**
("water heater repair near Leesburg," "well pump repair," "drain cleaning /
sewer line Loudoun," "emergency plumber") and on the **broad "Northern Virginia
plumber"** queries, **Artisan does not appear in the AI answer at all** —
while competitors with thinner reputations (Len The Plumber, Crown Plumber,
Stoney's, Priority 1, Roto-Rooter, Plumb Crazy, Veteran Plumbing) are named
repeatedly.

In short: **AI engines know Artisan is well-respected, but they don't recommend
you for the specific jobs customers are searching to hire for.** That gap is
fixable and it's where the revenue is.

## 1. Where Artisan already wins ✅

**Branded and reputation queries.** Ask an AI engine about "Artisan Plumbing"
or "best plumber in Loudoun County" and the response is accurate and
flattering. Engines correctly surface:

- Family / veteran-owned, established **2006** by David & Lisa Adams
- David Adams = master plumber, 25+ years
- **"Best of Loudoun" multiple years running**; **Northern Virginia Magazine
  2026 winner**
- Licensed, insured, 24/7, **non-commission / upfront pricing** (a real
  differentiator the AI repeats)
- One engine literally concluded *"Artisan Plumbing appears to stand out."*

These are excellent GEO assets. The award citations (NoVA Mag, Best of Loudoun,
Loudoun Chamber) are exactly the kind of third-party authority AI engines trust
— and they're currently doing the heavy lifting for your visibility.

**Implication:** your moat is reputation. The strategy is to extend that
reputation signal into the service/emergency queries where you're currently
silent — not to rebuild trust you already have.

## 2. Where Artisan is invisible ❌ (the revenue gap)

This is the core finding. Across the queries that represent **active hiring
intent**, Artisan was absent from the AI answer and competitors were named:

| Customer query (simulated) | Did Artisan appear? | Who the AI recommended instead |
|---|---|---|
| "best plumber in Northern Virginia / Fairfax / Arlington" | ❌ No | Pioneer, Len The Plumber, Morgan, DB's, Happy Drain Guys |
| "emergency plumber near Leesburg, water heater repair" | ❌ No | Len The Plumber, Roto-Rooter, Stoney's, Priority 1, Falcon |
| "licensed plumber Purcellville/Ashburn/Sterling, well pump repair" | ❌ No | Plumb Crazy, Veteran Plumbing, Tri-County, Casco |
| "drain cleaning / sewer line repair Loudoun County" | ❌ No | Len The Plumber, Crown, Stoney's, Priority 1 |
| "best plumber in **Loudoun County**" | ✅ Yes | (Artisan named and praised) |
| "Artisan Plumbing" (branded) | ✅ Yes | (Accurate) |

**Why this is happening.** AI answer engines assemble service recommendations
from pages that tightly match *service + location* intent — e.g. a page titled
"Water Heater Repair in Leesburg, VA." Your competitors have built dedicated,
well-structured **service-by-city pages** (Len The Plumber and Crown in
particular have one per service per town), so the engines have clean, citable
matches to pull. Artisan offers every one of these services — water heaters,
drain cleaning, well pumps, sewer locating/repair, leak detection — but that
content either isn't structured for extraction or isn't being cited.

You have *some* city pages (e.g. `/plumber-in-leesburg-va/`) and service pages
(residential, commercial), but they're not surfacing for the
service-specific questions. That's the gap to close.

**Why it matters commercially.** Emergency and specific-service queries are the
highest-conversion moments in the whole funnel — someone with a leaking water
heater at 9pm is ready to call *now*. Right now the AI hands that customer to
Len, Roto-Rooter, or Stoney's.

## 3. Citation & review footprint (the signals AI pulls from)

AI engines ground their answers in third-party citations. Here's Artisan's
current footprint:

| Source | Status | GEO impact |
|---|---|---|
| Northern Virginia Magazine (2026 winner) | ✅ Strong | High-authority, AI loves to cite — **leverage more** |
| Best of Loudoun (multi-year) | ✅ Strong | Repeatedly surfaced by engines |
| Loudoun Chamber / Regional Chamber | ✅ Present | Good local entity signal |
| Yelp | ✅ 4.4★ / ~35–36 reviews | Solid, frequently cited |
| Angi | ✅ 5.0 overall | Strong but lower citation weight |
| Google Business Profile | ⚠️ ~29 reviews | **Too thin** — Google review volume is the #1 lever for AI Overviews + Maps |
| BBB | ⚠️ **B-**, not accredited, 1 complaint | **Reputational drag** an AI may surface — needs attention |
| Nextdoor | ✅ 46 faves | Hyperlocal trust signal |
| Facebook / Yellow Pages / Manta / Alignable / Nextdoor | ✅ Present | Citation breadth is fine |

**Biggest review lever: Google.** ~29 Google reviews is low for a 20-year,
award-winning business — competitors carry hundreds. AI Overviews and
Maps-driven answers weight Google volume + recency heavily. Closing this single
gap would materially improve discovery-query visibility.

**BBB B- with an open complaint** is a liability: it's the one place an AI might
return a *negative* qualifier about you. Worth resolving / responding to.

## 4. Entity & NAP consistency ⚠️

AI engines build an internal "entity" for your business and get confused when
the Name/Address/Phone vary across the web. Inconsistencies found:

- **Name:** "Artisan Plumbing **Inc.**" (most listings) vs "Artisan Plumbing
  **LLC**" (Manta) vs "Artisan Plumbing" (Yelp/Nextdoor).
- **Address/City:** Leesburg (25 Catoctin Cir SE, Ste/PO 4434, 20175) vs
  **Purcellville** (Alignable) vs **Stephens City** (BBB, Yellow Pages).
- **Phone:** Two numbers appear in the wild — **540-868-0661** and a
  540-868 line — confirm one canonical number everywhere.

This fragmentation splits your authority across multiple half-entities and
weakens every query. **Pick one canonical NAP and enforce it** across GBP, BBB,
Yelp, chambers, and the website footer/schema.

## 5. On-page AI-readiness **[VERIFY with live crawl]**

Could not be crawled from our environment; confirm these and treat as the
likely technical to-do list:

- **[VERIFY] Structured data (Schema.org):** `Plumber` / `LocalBusiness`,
  `Service`, `FAQPage`, and `Review`/`AggregateRating` JSON-LD. This is how you
  hand AI engines clean, citable facts. Competitors winning service queries
  almost certainly have richer schema.
- **[VERIFY] FAQ content:** No dedicated FAQ/blog surfaced in research. FAQ
  pages ("How much does water heater replacement cost in Loudoun?", "Signs of
  a failing well pump") are *prime* AI-answer fuel — they match the
  question-shaped way people prompt.
- **[VERIFY] Service-by-city page depth:** Build/strengthen pages for each core
  service × each town you serve (Leesburg, Ashburn, Sterling, Purcellville,
  Hamilton, Lovettsville, South Riding, Aldie, Herndon, Winchester, Berryville,
  Charles Town).
- **[VERIFY] `robots.txt` / AI crawler access:** Confirm GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot,
  PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended are **not** blocked. (You generally
  *want* these engines crawling you.)
- **[VERIFY] `llms.txt`:** Not present at root in our checks. Adding a clean
  `/llms.txt` summarizing services, service area, hours, USPs, and contact gives
  engines a single canonical brief to quote.
- **[VERIFY] Page titles / H1s** explicitly combining service + city.

## 6. Competitor benchmark

Who is beating you *inside the AI answers* and why:

| Competitor | Why the AI keeps naming them |
|---|---|
| **Len The Plumber** | Dense service×city page network; 24/7 messaging; huge review volume; "serves 60,000 families/yr" stat the AI quotes |
| **Crown Plumber** | Dedicated sewer-line / service pages; same-day; free estimates phrasing |
| **Stoney's Plumbing** | Veteran-owned + master plumber story; warranty; strong local reviews |
| **Priority 1 / Plumb Crazy / Veteran Plumbing** | Hyper-local service-area pages naming every town; well-pump + sewer specificity |

The pattern is consistent: **they win on structured, service-specific,
town-named content + review volume.** Artisan wins on *reputation*. Combine
your reputation with their content structure and you out-rank all of them — you
have the better trust signals (NoVA Mag, Best of Loudoun) they lack.

## 7. Recommendations — prioritized

### P0 — Do in the next 30 days (high impact, low effort)
1. **Fix NAP/entity consistency.** Lock one legal name, one address, one phone;
   correct BBB, Manta, Alignable, Yelp, chambers, GBP, and site footer.
2. **Google review push.** Get from ~29 to 100+ via a simple post-job text/email
   ask. Biggest single lever for discovery-query visibility.
3. **Address the BBB B-.** Respond to the open complaint; pursue accreditation
   or at least a rating bump so AI has nothing negative to quote.
4. **Publish `/llms.txt`** with a canonical business brief (services, towns,
   hours, USPs, awards, contact).

### P1 — Next 60–90 days (high impact, moderate effort)
5. **Build the service × city page matrix.** One page per core service
   (water heater, drain cleaning, well pump, sewer line/locating, leak
   detection, emergency) × each served town. Title + H1 = "Service in City, VA."
   This directly targets the queries you're currently invisible for.
6. **Add an FAQ hub** with question-shaped headings and concise answers;
   mark up with `FAQPage` schema.
7. **Deploy full LocalBusiness/Plumber + Service + AggregateRating JSON-LD**
   sitewide.

### P2 — Ongoing (compounding)
8. **Leverage the awards on-page** — a dedicated "Awards & Recognition" page
   citing NoVA Mag 2026, Best of Loudoun (each year), Chamber membership gives
   AI engines authoritative anchors to cite.
9. **Light content cadence** — short, helpful posts on common Loudoun plumbing
   problems (hard water, well systems, winter pipe burst) to keep feeding
   answer engines fresh, citable Q&A.
10. **Re-audit quarterly** — re-run the query set to track movement from "D" to
    "B+" on service/emergency intent.

### The 30-day quick-win bundle
Fix NAP → push Google reviews → resolve BBB → ship `llms.txt` + schema → stand
up the top 3 service×city pages (water heater, drain/sewer, well pump for
Leesburg + Purcellville + Ashburn). That alone should start putting Artisan into
the service-intent answers where the money is.
